#### Applied Deliverable

Sarah, I am ready to contribute to business intelligence work at Southwest because I have used data to turn a business problem into a supported recommendation. In Flex 4, I created the **Inside Sales Service Cost** view, which showed that the tier cost **$143,600** to support **359 accounts** while producing a net contribution loss of **$16,821.86**. I would rebuild this view in Power BI using conditional formatting so the loss appears in red while profitable tiers remain neutral. This would help Marcus identify the problem tier immediately. I also created a **Net Contribution** calculation by subtracting cost to serve from profit. In Power BI, I would build it as a DAX measure so Anita or Marcus could filter by tier or account and receive updated results automatically.

I would use Excel for data cleaning, quick calculations, and one-time analyses. I would use Tableau for polished public visualizations and visual stories. I would use Power BI for internal dashboards that require reusable measures, connected data, interactive filtering, and regular updates. My main experience gap is that I have not published and maintained a Power BI report that employees depend on. I would address this gap by publishing a practice report to Power BI Service, testing its permissions and scheduled refreshes, and asking users to test it before treating it as a production report.

## Adobe Express Work Sample

### November 2, 2026 — Data Storytelling with Adobe Express

This work sample was prepared for **Jordan Ellis, Vice President of Card Growth at Bear Down Bank**. It recommends launching the Wildcat Rewards Card pilot in Texas and Colorado, where **81 of 182 customers qualify**, producing a **44.51% eligibility rate—2.19 times the nationwide rate**.

- **Adobe Express project:** [View the published infographic](https://new.express.adobe.com/publishedV2/urn:aaid:sc:US:6c39075b-b8ae-54e7-9425-c6dc2f9e8208?promoid=Y69SGM5H&sdid=C4SZ2FYJ&mv=other)
- **Work sample PDF:** [View the PDF](https://github.com/GC23-Stack/mis561-portfolio/blob/main/adobe/chavez-data-storytelling.pdf)
- **Training completed:** November 2, 2026

#### Design Choice

I used a benchmarked bar chart because Jordan needs to compare Texas and Colorado directly with the **20.35% nationwide eligibility rate** while also seeing the number of qualified customers. I considered using a state map but rejected it because differences in geographic size would make the eligibility-rate comparison less precise. I removed the generation and FICO breakdowns from the exploratory dashboard so the final infographic remains focused on the pilot-location decision.
